📘 How Focus Score is Calculated

📈 Focus percentage is calculated in real-time using your activity.

🧠 Formula used:

Focus % = (Active Time ÷ Total Time) × 100

Example:

  • Focused time → 80 seconds
  • Distracted time → 20 seconds
  • Total time → 100 seconds

Final Calculation:

(80 ÷ 100) × 100 = 80%

⚡ This value is updated every few seconds and stored daily in the database.
